## Distinct question and newly available evidence

The 2026-10-26 K-provider evidence-request packet and 2026-10-28 issuer-role audit established that official BOAT RACE download navigation and organization-level roles do not identify a K-host-specific licensing/attestation authority. A **newly launched third-party results database (2026-10-26)** now publicly describes its official-file ingestion and permissive research-facing API terms. Does that independent *operator* supply independent K *issuer* rights, authenticated correction completeness, or source finality? **No such authority is evidenced by the reviewed pages.** This audit classifies the new candidate without attempting the already-blocked K-page extraction.

Public pages inspected on 2026-10-31 JST:

1. [艇愛ボートレースDB, data and terms](https://boatrace-db.jp/about/data/) and [launch notice](https://boatrace-db.jp/): operator states that its figures derive from BOAT RACE official downloadable result/program/racer files, with intraday results subsequently overwritten by the nightly official result file. It describes periodic re-fetch of recent days and offers its **own** API for personal/research use subject to attribution, request-rate and redistribution conditions. It explicitly asks users to compare with organizer announcements and disclaims accuracy responsibility. This is a **derived secondary publication**, not an attributable K archive rights grant, signed revision stream, issuer-controlled checkpoint, or exact-ticket refund/finality authority. The operator's permission for its own API does **not** confer upstream K-host rights.
2. [BoatraceCSV](https://boatracecsv.github.io/): publicly offers derived daily/realtime CSV results and payouts, with an MIT license stated on its site. The site does not establish that the official K issuer grants rights to the upstream K archive, nor does the software/site license establish authenticated official correction order or completeness. No license inheritance is inferred.
3. [Boatrace Open API for Results](https://github.com/lamrongol/BoatraceResults): describes itself as unofficial, with approximately periodic updates and no accuracy/completeness guarantee. A periodically republished JSON snapshot cannot independently prove an issuer-authenticated inclusive correction history.

**Novel operational distinction:** A third-party source can be independently *operated* yet remain **downstream of the same official source**, and can expose an accessible/research-permitted derivative without granting rights to the original archive. Its polling/replacement timestamps are not source-controlled revision sequence or a cryptographically authenticated finality checkpoint. Multiple derived mirrors of the same upstream file are not independent attestations of omitted corrections.
